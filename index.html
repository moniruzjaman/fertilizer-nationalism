Stage 1 · The ground truth

The char remembers every flood — and plants anyway.

Sixteen rivers cross Kurigram. When upstream rains fall, the water can rise 50 centimetres in 24 hours, taking roofs, seeds and savings in a single night. And when it retreats, it leaves behind the silt — golden, fertile, forgiving — and 5 lakh farming families walk back into the field before the mud is dry. This is not a deficit district. This is the district that turned monga into surplus. The proposal below is simply the state walking back into the field with them.

"The river took our roof in the night. By morning the silt was still warm — and we were already planting. We do not ask for charity. We ask for fertiliser at the price the government printed, on the day the season needs it."

Stage 2 · Diagnosis

The warehouse is full. The farmer's hand is not. Between them: leakage.

Nationally, Tk 17,001 crore of subsidy — ~80% of public agricultural spending — flows through a channel where the bottom 40% of farmers receive only ~15% of the benefit. In Kurigram the same disease shows three symptoms: price premiums, unlifted allocations, and over-application driven by guesswork.

Stage 4 · The instrument

Four layers, one grid, one package, one char promise.

1 Identity & Land — the Farmer Card completes here
100% enrolment of 566,202 farm families (508,493 + 57,709 sharecroppers) on the Krishak/Farmer Card, biometrically linked to NID and BDS/EDLMS parcels via Union Parishad single-window desks.
120 data points/farmerGPMS baseline 0.02 lakh → 5.66 lakh22M national IDs by 2028Korea MoU land digitization
2 Point of Sale — fingerprint match, offline-first
116 biometric PoS terminals at 107 outlets (76 union/paurashava dealers under the 2026 Dealer Policy + 31 char hubs) + 9 spares; 9 upazila local-server nodes + district mirror so char sales never stop for a network outage.
Authentication = fingerprint match (PoS + local server)+ crop declaration & land validationBangla voice UI
3 Tracking & Transparency — the real-time audit
Central Digital Ledger with permissioned blockchain: every kilogram traced import → warehouse → dealer → farmer; public dashboard; automated leakage alerts; quarterly public reports to the Manifesto Implementation Committee.
Real-time auditPerformance audit (3rd party)Open e-GP tendering
4 Advisory & Verification — the Soil Health Grid
Not blanket cards. A land-based grid: 5,676 plain samples (1/20 ha) + 4,403 char samples (1/10 ha) + 1,008 QC = 11,087 lab tests, generating 436,902 parcel-linked digital Soil Health Cards for every landholding family. National scale-up formula: same 20 ha/10 ha grid ≈ 400–450k tests, not millions of physical cards.
SRDI full-profile @ Tk 1,500Digital card @ Tk 40 marginalSatellite NDVI verificationUSSD/SMS advisory
